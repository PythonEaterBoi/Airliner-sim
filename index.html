HOW TO FLY

Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left half = control column (roll/pitch).
Right edge: vertical slider = thrust (drag below 0 for reverse on the ground).
Orange slider = rudder / nose-wheel steering. TRIM relieves column force.
Takeoff: thrust slider to 100 %, rotate at ~145 kt (pull stick down-back), GEAR up at 400 ft, climb at 160–180 kt.
Landing: join the ILS with APP (or hand-fly the approach lights), GEAR down by 1000 ft, flaps 15–30, ~130–140 kt, flare at 30 ft, idle, brakes + SPDBRK after touchdown.
Keyboard: arrows fly (up = nose up), Q/E rudder, Shift/Ctrl thrust, G gear, F/R flaps, S speedbrake, B brakes, V camera, A autopilot, Z autothrottle, X approach arm, T trim, P park brake, M menu.
Every pixel of the outside view is rendered in Python (numpy software rasteriser) on the server and streamed to this device as JPEG.
